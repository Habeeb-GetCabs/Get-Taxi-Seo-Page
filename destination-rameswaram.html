Rameswaram & Dhanushkodi — Destination & Travel Guide

Sacred island pilgrimage destination connected to mainland India by the iconic Pamban Bridge, housing Sri Ramanathaswamy Jyotirlinga Temple (22 holy theerthams), Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Memorial, and Dhanushkodi.

Highway Corridor Distance from Coimbatore: ~385 KM (Indicative) Approx. Drive Time: Approx. 7.5 Hours via Dharapuram, Madurai & Ramanathapuram (NH87) Best Season: October to April

Visitor Overview & Highway Corridor

Primary Route: Coimbatore → Madurai → Paramakudi → Ramanathapuram → Pamban Bridge → Rameswaram

Most families combine Madurai Meenakshi Temple and Rameswaram in a 2-to-3 day pilgrimage cab tour from Coimbatore.

Top Places to Visit & Attractions in Rameswaram & Dhanushkodi

★ Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple (1,200m Pillared Corridor & 22 Theerthams)
★ Agni Theertham Beach
★ Pamban Road & Rail Bridges
★ Dhanushkodi Beach & Arichal Munai (Land’s End)
★ Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam National Memorial, Peikarumbu

Transportation & Access Notes

170 km southeast of Madurai on NH87 across Pamban Bridge.
