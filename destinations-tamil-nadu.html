Coimbatore District (Kongu Nadu)

Coimbatore (Kovai)

The second-largest city in Tamil Nadu and gateway to the Western Ghats, known for Isha Yoga Adiyogi, Maruthamalai & Perur temples, textile & engineering industry, super-specialty hospitals, and Nilgiri hill access.

Route: NH544, NH81, NH83, NH181 & NH948 Junction

Top Attractions: Isha Yoga Center & 112ft Adiyogi, Maruthamalai Murugan Temple, Perur Pateeswarar Temple, Gedee Car Museum

Ooty (Udhagamandalam)

The "Queen of Hill Stations" perched at 2,240 meters in the Nilgiri Biosphere, celebrated for Government Botanical Gardens, Doddabetta Peak, Ooty Lake, Rose Garden, and the UNESCO Nilgiri Mountain Railway.

Route: Coimbatore → Mettupalayam → Coonoor / Kotagiri → Ooty (NH181)

Kodaikanal

Route: Coimbatore → Pollachi → Palani → Perumal Malai → Kodaikanal

Valparai

An unspoiled, lush green hill station at 3,500 feet in the Anamalai mountain range of Coimbatore district, reached via 40 thrilling hairpin bends overlooking Aliyar Reservoir and Lion-Tailed Macaque habitats.

Palani

Madurai

One of the world’s oldest continuously inhabited cities ("Thoonga Nagaram"), anchored by the magnificent Arulmigu Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple on the banks of the Vaigai River.

Top Attractions: Meenakshi Amman Temple, Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace, Alagar Koyil & Pazhamudhircholai (6th Abode), Thiruparankundram Murugan Temple (1st Abode)

Tiruppur

Known globally as India’s "Dollar City" and knitwear capital, Tiruppur has constant daily business, buyer, and airport transfer traffic with Coimbatore International Airport (only 46 km away).

Erode

Salem

Fifth-largest urban agglomeration in Tamil Nadu surrounded by hills, famous for Salem Steel Plant, Kottai Mariamman & Sugavaneswarar temples, Mettur Dam, and as the foothill base for Yercaud.

Yercaud

Peaceful coffee and orange plantation hill station at 1,515 meters in the Shevaroy Hills above Salem, reached via a well-paved 20-hairpin-bend ghat road.

Top Attractions: Yercaud Emerald Lake & Boathouse, Lady’s Seat, Gent’s Seat & Children’s Seat Viewpoints, Pagoda Point, Shevaroy Temple & Servarayan Cave

Tiruchirappalli District (Central Tamil Nadu)

Tiruchirappalli (Trichy)

Situated on the banks of the Kaveri River, Trichy is home to the world’s largest functioning Hindu temple complex—Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple at Srirangam—and the iconic Rockfort Ucchi Pillayar Temple.

Route: Coimbatore → Singanallur → Palladam → Kangeyam → Karur → Trichy (NH81)

Top Attractions: Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple, Srirangam, Rockfort Ucchi Pillayar Temple, Thiruvanaikaval Jambukeswarar Temple (Pancha Bhoota Water), Samayapuram Mariamman Temple

Thanjavur District (Cauvery Delta)

Thanjavur (Tanjore)

Historic capital of the Imperial Cholas, home to the 1,010-year-old UNESCO World Heritage Brihadeeswarar Temple (Big Temple) built by Raja Raja Chola I, Saraswathi Mahal Library, and Tanjore Maratha Palace.

Route: Coimbatore → Karur → Trichy → Sengipatti → Thanjavur

Top Attractions: Brihadeeswarar Temple (Peruvudaiyar Kovil UNESCO Site), Thanjavur Maratha Palace & Art Gallery, Saraswathi Mahal Library, Swamimalai Murugan Temple (4th Abode, 32 km)

Thanjavur District (Cauvery Delta)

Kumbakonam

Celebrated as the "City of Temples" between the Kaveri and Arasalar rivers, serving as the central base for the 9 Navagraha (Planetary Deity) temples, Adi Kumbeswarar, Sarangapani, and Swamimalai shrines.

Route: Coimbatore → Karur → Trichy → Thanjavur → Kumbakonam

Top Attractions: Adi Kumbeswarar & Sarangapani Temples, Mahamaham Tank, Swamimalai Murugan Temple, Darasuram Airavatesvara UNESCO Temple

Ramanathapuram District

Rameswaram & Dhanushkodi

Sacred island pilgrimage destination connected to mainland India by the iconic Pamban Bridge, housing Sri Ramanathaswamy Jyotirlinga Temple (22 holy theerthams), Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Memorial, and Dhanushkodi.

Route: Coimbatore → Madurai → Paramakudi → Ramanathapuram → Pamban Bridge → Rameswaram

Top Attractions: Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple (1,200m Pillared Corridor & 22 Theerthams), Agni Theertham Beach, Pamban Road & Rail Bridges, Dhanushkodi Beach & Arichal Munai (Land’s End)

Kanyakumari District (Southernmost Tip of Mainland India)

Kanyakumari

The southernmost tip of mainland India where the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, and Indian Ocean meet, famous for Vivekananda Rock Memorial, the 133-foot Thiruvalluvar Statue, Bhagavathy Amman Temple, and ocean sunrises.

Route: Coimbatore → Madurai → Tirunelveli → Nagercoil → Kanyakumari (NH44)

Top Attractions: Vivekananda Rock Memorial & Glass Bridge, 133-ft Thiruvalluvar Statue, Arulmigu Bhagavathy Amman Temple, Triveni Sangamam Sunrise & Sunset Viewpoint

Tiruvannamalai District

Tiruvannamalai

Sacred spiritual city at the base of Annamalai Hill, home to the 24-acre Arulmigu Arunachaleswarar Temple (Fire element of Pancha Bhoota Stalam), Sri Ramana Ashram, and the 14-km Girivalam circumambulation path.

Route: Coimbatore → Salem → Harur / Singarapettai → Chengam → Tiruvannamalai

Top Attractions: Arulmigu Arunachaleswarar Temple, Sri Ramana Ashram, 14-km Annamalai Hill Girivalam Path (8 Ashta Lingams), Virupaksha Cave & Skandashramam

Dindigul District

Dindigul

Historic town dominated by the 17th-century Dindigul Rock Fort, famous for GI-tagged Dindigul locks, Seeraga Samba Thalappakatti biryani, Sirumalai hills, and as the main highway junction for Kodaikanal and Madurai.

Route: Coimbatore → Palladam → Dharapuram → Oddanchatram → Dindigul

Top Attractions: Dindigul Rock Fort (Malaikottai), Abirami Amman Temple, Soundararaja Perumal Temple, Thadikombu, Sirumalai Hill Station (25 km)

Cuddalore District

Chidambaram & Pichavaram Mangroves

Home to the ancient Thillai Nataraja Temple (representing Akasha/Space among the Pancha Bhoota Stalams) and located just 15 km from Pichavaram, the world’s second-largest mangrove forest with boat rides.

Route: Coimbatore → Salem → Attur → Vriddhachalam → Chidambaram

Top Attractions: Thillai Nataraja Temple, Chidambaram, Thillai Kali Amman Temple, Pichavaram Mangrove Forest Boating (TTDC Boathouse), Annamalai University Campus

Vellore & Tirupattur Districts (Northern Tamil Nadu)

Vellore & Yelagiri Hills

Major northern Tamil Nadu corridor covering Christian Medical College (CMC) Vellore, the 16th-century Vellore Fort & Jalakandeswarar Temple, Sripuram Golden Temple, and the family-friendly Yelagiri Hill Station (14 hairpin bends near Jolarpettai).

Route: Coimbatore → Salem → Krishnagiri → Tirupattur (Yelagiri) → Ambur → Vellore

Top Attractions: Vellore Fort & Jalakandeswarar Temple, Sripuram Mahalakshmi Golden Temple, Christian Medical College (CMC) Vellore, Yelagiri Hills (Punganoor Lake, Nature Park & Swamimalai Hills)

UNESCO 7th-Century Pallava Rock-Cut Monuments 495 KM

Chengalpattu District (Coromandel Coast)

Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram)

UNESCO World Heritage coastal town built by the Pallava kings in the 7th and 8th centuries, famous for the Shore Temple overlooking the Bay of Bengal, Pancha Rathas (Five Rathas), Arjuna’s Penance bas-relief, and Krishna’s Butterball.

Route: Coimbatore → Salem → Tindivanam → Chengalpattu → Mamallapuram

Top Attractions: Shore Temple (UNESCO Site), Pancha Rathas (Five Monolithic Chariots), Arjuna’s Penance & Krishna’s Butterball, Mahabalipuram Lighthouse & Beach

Nellaiappar Temple, Port City & Tiruchendur Sea-Shore Murugan Shrine 375 KM

Tirunelveli & Thoothukudi Districts (Southern Tamil Nadu)

Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi & Tiruchendur

Southern Tamil Nadu heritage belt encompassing Tirunelveli (Nellaiappar Temple, Tamirabarani River, GI-tagged Iruttukadai Halwa, and Courtallam waterfalls nearby), Thoothukudi port city (Our Lady of Snows Basilica), and Tiruchendur Subramaniya Swamy Temple (2nd Abode of Lord Murugan on the seashore).

Route: Coimbatore → Madurai → Virudhunagar → Kovilpatti → Tirunelveli / Thoothukudi / Tiruchendur

Top Attractions: Arulmigu Subramaniya Swamy Temple, Tiruchendur (2nd Abode), Nellaiappar Gandhimathi Amman Temple, Tirunelveli, Our Lady of Snows Basilica, Thoothukudi, Courtallam Waterfalls (Tenkasi — 60 km from Tirunelveli)
