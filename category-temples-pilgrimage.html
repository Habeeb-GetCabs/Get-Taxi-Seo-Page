Spiritual & Meditation Center

Velliangiri Foothills, Ishana Vihar · Coimbatore

Isha Yoga Center & 112-ft Adiyogi Statue

World-renowned yoga and meditation sanctuary at the foothills of the Velliangiri Mountains, home to Dhyanalinga, Linga Bhairavi, Theerthakunds, and the 112-foot Adiyogi Shiva bust.

Address: Velliangiri Foothills, Ishana Vihar Post, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 641114

Hours: 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Campus & Adiyogi open daily)

Ganesha Temple (Highway Shrine)

Eachanari (Pollachi Main Road) · Coimbatore

Arulmigu Eachanari Vinayagar Temple

Dating back to 1500 CE, this revered highway temple houses a 6-foot tall idol of Lord Vinayagar and is the traditional blessing stop for travellers heading to Pollachi, Palani, and Valparai.

Address: NH 83, Pollachi Main Road, Eachanari, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 641021

Hours: 5:00 AM – 1:00 PM & 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Verification: Verified (2026-10-01) · TN HR&CE Official Temple Records
