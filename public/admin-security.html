How to Stop Competitor Click Waste in Google Ads (Guaranteed Steps)

Apply these 4 campaign configurations immediately in your Google Ads account

Step 1 • The #1 Budget Drain Fix

Change Location Targeting to "Presence Only"

By default, Google Ads sets location targeting to: "Presence or interest: People in, or who have shown interest in your location". This causes competitors and users outside Coimbatore to see your ads and click them.

Campaign Settings ➔ Locations ➔ Location Options ➔ Select: "Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted location"
Step 2 • Add IP Exclusions

Paste Suspicious IPs into Google Ads Exclusions

Once an IP is added to your Campaign Exclusions list, Google will never serve your ads to that person again, saving your click budget.

Campaign Settings ➔ Additional Settings ➔ IP Exclusions ➔ Click 'Edit' and paste the IPs copied from this dashboard.
Step 3 • Check Automated Refunds

Verify Google's "Invalid Clicks" Credit Column

Google Ads has an automated multi-stage fraud filter that automatically credits back clicks it deems fraudulent (e.g. rapid repeat clicks from same competitor).

In Google Ads: Columns ➔ Modify Columns ➔ Performance ➔ Check 'Invalid Clicks' & 'Invalid Click Rate'. You will see refunded amounts!
Step 4 • Negative Keywords

Block Non-Customer Intent Searches

Rival cab drivers searching for taxi attachments, driver jobs, and vehicle permits click ads thinking it is a fleet portal.

Add Negative Keywords: [driver job], [taxi attachment], [cab driver vacancy], [uber driver], [ola driver], [rto permit].
